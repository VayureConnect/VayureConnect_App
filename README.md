Vayure Connect

Tagline: Breathe Easier

**About the App**

Vayure Connect is a prototype mobile app designed to work with the physical Vayure device. Together, they can provide personalised health insights based on your location and environmental conditions.

This app is currently an prototype and serves as a demonstration of how the device and app would work together.

Combining locational data and air quality measurements to deliver real-time updates, the goal of this app is to help you make informed decisions about your wellbeing.

This is an early prototype, developed as a demonstration of the app half of the concept.



**Features**

Real-time air quality updates.

Location-based personalised insights.

Air quality data: barometric pressure, carbon dioxide (CO2) levels, as well as carbon monoxide (CO) measurements.

Simplistic user-friendly interface.

Secure handling of personal and device data.



**Notes**

Built as a SwiftUI iOS prototype.

Demonstrates navigation flows, splash screen, and core concept UI.

Uses simulated and third-party environmental data sources (Apple maps).

Not yet connected to production APIs.



**Installation**

As this is a prototype:

Clone/download the project.

Open in Xcode (latest version recommended).

Build & run on simulator or device.
